# PhishGuard — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Applies to PhishGuard version 3.5.1 and later_

PhishGuard is a browser extension that looks for phishing warning signs on the websites you visit. All checks run on your own device. PhishGuard has no server, and it does not send your data to the developer or to anyone else.

## What PhishGuard looks at

To check a page, PhishGuard reads, on your device only:

- **The address (URL) of the page** you are on, and of links you hover over.
- **The structure of sign-in forms**: whether a page has a password field, and the web address that a form says it will send to.
- **Visible page text on pages that ask for a password**, only to look for a fixed list of pressure phrases such as "account suspended" or "verify immediately".

PhishGuard **never reads what you type** into any field, including passwords. It does not record keystrokes, take screenshots, read cookies, or submit forms.

## What PhishGuard stores

PhishGuard saves the following in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on this device:

- Your settings (whether warnings are on or off).
- The hostnames you chose to trust.
- A short scan history: up to 25 entries, each with a website's hostname, the result, the score, how many checks ran, and the time.

This data stays on your device. You can delete the history at any time in **History → Clear history**, and remove trusted sites in **Settings**. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.

## What PhishGuard shares

Nothing. PhishGuard does not transmit, sell, or share any data, and it uses no analytics, advertising, or third-party services. It does not download threat lists or remote code.

If you choose **Contact developer**, PhishGuard opens a draft in your own email app with the text you wrote. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

## Permissions

- **Access to websites (`<all_urls>`)**: needed to check the address and sign-in forms of the pages you visit and to show a warning on risky pages.
- **Storage**: keeps your settings, trusted sites and scan history on your device.
- **Scripting**: lets PhishGuard start checking tabs that were already open when it was installed or updated.

## Children

PhishGuard is a general-purpose security tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
