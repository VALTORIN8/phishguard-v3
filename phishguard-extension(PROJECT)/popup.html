Why these permissions?

PhishGuard needs access to pages to inspect the current URL, password-form structure, and warning signals. It does not read typed passwords. It no longer requests the separate “tabs” permission.

Website accessInspect URLs and form metadata on HTTP(S) pages; may show an in-page warning.
StorageKeep your on/off setting, trusted hostnames, and local scan history.
ScriptingReconnect inspection on tabs open before installing or reloading the extension.

Chrome's <all_urls> permission is broad; this feature requires access to pages you visit. Disable or uninstall the extension to revoke this access.

Trusted sites

Trusting a hostname suppresses PhishGuard warnings on that exact hostname (not its subdomains). A trusted site is not necessarily safe. Scans stay available.
